Indian / French Winter School on Artificial Intelligence and Remote Sensing for Mountain Cryosphere
11–22 January 2027 | Indian Institute of Remote Sensing (IIRS), ISRO, Dehradun, India

The winter school is structured across two intensive weeks, combining remote sensing fundamentals for mountain hazards with advanced machine learning and AI applications for Earth Observation.

WEEK 1: Mountain Hazard & Cryosphere Remote Sensing

Focuses on sensor modalities, Earth observation data acquisition, and physical retrieval techniques for cryospheric dynamics.

Day Topic & Technical Focus
DAY 1
Multispectral / Hyperspectral Imagery for snow, glacier & high-altitude lake monitoring - Dr. A. V. Kulkarni (IISc Bangalore)
Applications for snow characterization, glacier mapping, and high-altitude lake monitoring.
DAY 2
Thermal Imagery for cryosphere & permafrost studies - Dr. Sushil Kuamr (Space Applications Center)
Thermal remote sensing methodology for cryosphere, permafrost, and surface energy balance studies.
DAY 3
SAR / PolSAR Imagery for snow cover, glacier lake & avalanche monitoring - Dr. Avik Bhattacharia (IIT-B) + ??
High-resolution elevation modeling, topographic mapping, and elevation change detection.
DAY 4
SAR / InSAR measurement of glacier & permafrost displacement, snow depth & SWE- Dr. Praveen Thakur, Sakshi and Apoorva
Polarimetric SAR for snow cover analysis, glacial lake monitoring, and avalanche hazard tracking.
DAY 5
Measurement of glacier & permafrost elevation and displacement - Dr. Emmanuel Trouvé and Dr. Laurane, Dr. Adina and Dr. Amaury -
Measuring glacier velocity, permafrost displacement, snow depth, and Snow Water Equivalent (SWE).
DAY 6
Fieldwork — Snow & Landslide - Dr. Praveen Thakur
On-site field measurement techniques and validation protocols in the Himalayan foothill region.

WEEK 2: Advances in AI for Earth Observation

Focuses on state-of-the-art machine learning algorithms, deep neural networks, physics-informed AI, and multi-modal fusion.

Day Topic & Technical Focus
DAY 1
AI basics
Automated co-registration, feature matching, and SAR despeckling using deep learning models.
DAY 2
Deep learning for land use & land cover classification - Dr. Anil Kumar (IIRS, Dehradun)
Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Transformers for land use / land cover (LULC) classification.
DAY 3
Database and machine learning for landform motion detection by InSAR - Dr. Antoine Bralet (ICube, Strasbourg)
Automated detection and time-series extraction of landform motion and slope instability from InSAR.
DAY 4
Optimisation and AI models for source separation & multi-modal fusion - Dr. Argheesh Bhanot (LISTIC, USMB)
Signal unmixing, multi-sensor data fusion (optical + SAR + elevation), and convex optimization.
