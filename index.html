Course Modules Overview

WEEK 1

Mountain Hazard & Cryosphere Remote Sensing

Covers multispectral, thermal, LiDAR, photogrammetry, SAR/PolSAR, and InSAR techniques alongside a field trip.

WEEK 2

Advances in AI for Earth Observation

Covers AI data prep, deep learning for LULC, ML for InSAR, source separation, PINNs, and GNNs.
